This website uses [GoatCounter](https://www.goatcounter.com/), a privacy-friendly, open-source web analytics tool. 

### What is collected
GoatCounter is designed to track basic traffic metrics without identifying individual visitors:
- **Page path** requested
- **Referrer** URL (where you came from)
- **Browser and operating system** user-agent string
- **Country code** derived from your IP address

### What is NOT collected
- **No cookies** are set or stored in your browser.
- **No persistent identifiers** or unique user IDs are tracked across visits.
- **No IP addresses** are permanently stored or logged (IP addresses are anonymized and hashed transiently to count unique visitors without retaining personal data).

### Purpose
Data collected through GoatCounter is used solely to measure the imapct of the MOLE course.
We don't use any personal information or save IP addresses. 
Any location information is aggregated at the State/Province level and binned further if certain locales have small traffic.
We will publish site traffic to [mole-logistics](https://github.com/molevolworkshop/mole-logistics).

If you have any questions regarding privacy practices on this site, feel free to reach out.
